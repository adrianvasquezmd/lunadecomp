# lunadecomp 0.1.1

Initial publication.

* Oaxaca-Blinder and Yun decomposition of two-group outcome gaps using OLS,
  linear probability, logit, and probit models, with alternative reference
  structures and categorical normalization.
* Fairlie decomposition of binary outcome gaps, with grouped predictors,
  weighted rank matching, and matching-precision diagnostics.
* Wagstaff, van Doorslaer, and Watanabe decomposition of concentration indices,
  with standard, generalized, Erreygers, and Wagstaff corrections.
* Kessels-Erreygers direct regression for rank- and level-dependent inequality
  indices, with coefficient inference and variable-level importance measures.
* Survey weights, strata, PSUs, and explicit analytical domains through
  `analysis_subset`. Linearized, jackknife, and bootstrap inference are
  available subject to each method's documented assumptions.
* Reproducible examples, simulated data, and help for each method.
* AGPL version 3 or later, with implementation credits and third-party notices.
